NIN Verification Guidelines

NIN Verification 1: If you recently modified your NIN details and it has reflected on the immigration portal, you can use the NIN verification 1 option. Kindly note: A slip is saved to history only when you download any available slip after a successful verification. Slips remain in history for 3 days and are then cleared automatically.

NIN Verification 2: If you want access to various slips like VNIN and other custom templates distinct from Verification 1, use the NIN verification 2 option. After a successful verification, the selected slip stays in your history table so you can return to download it later.

Important Notice: You will NOT be debited if your transaction fails. If it ever appears you were debited due to a network glitch, please check your history table before attempting another verification.
